HOW TO READ THIS

Counts ≠ spoken mentions

A projected count is an average. 2+ is the chance of at least two made alley-oops—not two mentions.

The separate broadcast layer recovers two versions of Arena’s booth assumptions. Without a source-documented crew and matching contract feed, it shows assumption sensitivity only. Its range is not a confidence interval.

Count thresholds are calibrated against made alley-oops. The broadcast conversion is an unvalidated heuristic and must not be treated as a validated trading signal.

Kalshi mention quotes are not connected. No quotes, fabricated contracts, or model-versus-market edges are published.
