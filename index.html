HOW TO READ THIS

Match the outcome to the market

Broadcast mentions: Yes means the eligible announcers say the phrase within the specified window. A made alley-oop does not guarantee a mention. Crew and contract rules matter.

Made counts: Over 1.5 means at least two made alley-oops; Under 1.5 means zero or one. The model's threshold rows are not sportsbook lines. Team probabilities refer to that team's makes.

Mention quotes use Yes/No bid and ask prices. Count markets use a named bookmaker, outcome, line and American odds. Each quote has its own retrieval time; stale quotes are marked.

Count thresholds use out-of-fold calibration and stated count-distribution assumptions. Broadcast scenarios are unvalidated and are not confidence intervals.
