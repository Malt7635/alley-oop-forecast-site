HOW TO READ THIS

Counts & probabilities

A projected count is an average, not a chance of an alley-oop. 2+ means the probability of at least two made alley-oops.

The model predicts each team’s chance of any made alley-oop and its positive count. Higher thresholds use a stated count-shape assumption; the game total assumes independent team counts.

A sportsbook line is shown only when a provider explicitly publishes a genuine alley-oop prop market. Ordinary basketball totals are never treated as alley-oop lines.
